Variant E — zoom levels (mechanic only)

E What scroll/zoom feels like — five levels, each is a different layout

Hour

Single event, full Wikipedia paragraph + image + links. The summary view.

Day

Brick layout (Variant C). 8-20 events, weighted by spike score.

Week

Vertical timeline (Variant B). One day-row, top 3-5 events per day.

Month

Density grid: 28-31 columns of days, top entities stacked by colour. Click any cell → day.

Year

Year strip (Variant D). One bar per entity per month, height = activity.

Mouse-wheel or pinch-zoom transitions between adjacent levels. Each level's "click" zooms in one step. Browser back/forward maps to zoom history. URL is stable per level: /news, /news/2026-05-25, /news/2026-W21, etc.

Strategic positioning

The product positioning question is upstream of the layout. Four options on the spectrum:

  1. Search engine with a news rail — today. Search owns the page.
  2. Search engine with a big news strip — Variant A. Search still owns the homepage; news is now twice the size.
  3. News + search — Variants B / C. Equal weight. Homepage is "today's events"; search is one keystroke away. Recommendation if we ship this.
  4. News with search hidden — Variants B / C with the toolbar collapsing to an icon. Repositions the product. Search becomes a power-user feature. Higher reward, higher risk.

The brand promise to articulate: "the news, as Wikipedia tells it" — slower than newspapers, neutralised by the editing process, dated and sourced. Not breaking news; news with a week of perspective.
