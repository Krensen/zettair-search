The news, as Wikipedia tells it

A daily timeline of events from Wikipedia article paragraphs — slower than newspapers, sourced and dated. Search too.

Events on this page are dated paragraphs extracted from Wikipedia articles when those articles trended. The text is what Wikipedia editors wrote; we are surfacing it, not summarising it. Some events fall back to news headlines when Wikipedia has not yet documented them, in which case the source is marked.
